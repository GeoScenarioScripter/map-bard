# Cartography

Keep the claim visible: use a common scale for scenes being compared, show units, and check the resulting legend in the preview. Set a camera that includes the intended features and enough geographic context to orient readers. Place annotations where they do not cover their targets. Choose `labels: minimal` or `off` when basemap labels compete with thematic data. Keep source and media credit visible. Online public tile services have no uptime guarantee; include local context layers when offline reading matters.

Heights are linear from zero. A column's footprint is constant, not measured area or volume. Keep a common `maxHeightMeters` across compared scenes; increasing it to improve readability must affect both periods. Use a pitched camera where height carries the claim (`DEPTH_UNSEEN` warns when the camera never pitches). Polygon extrusions require an already-normalized ratio and a real denominator; the engine does not calculate that normalization for you. State the normalization, since large polygons can still dominate attention.

Change symbols retain the before state as a ring or translucent ghost and show the after state as a disc or solid column. Match the legend dates to the observations. Inspect ghost contrast on the actual basemap and the smallest after columns on a phone. Do not impose a nonzero minimum height or change the scale per period to make a decline look better. Generated labels state absolute and percentage change; put the individual observed values in narration or authored labels when small marks are hard to read.

Disclose hexbin cell size, aggregation operation and missing values. Hexbins use a Web Mercator grid: ground areas change with latitude. `ENCODING_AREA_DISTORTED` warns above 10% area variation; narrow the latitude span or split scenes rather than implying equal-density cells. OD arcs indicate relationships; their arch height is decorative. Trips interpolate between recorded vertices, so the path between observations is not extra evidence. Keep margins of error and source qualifications visible in flow stories.
